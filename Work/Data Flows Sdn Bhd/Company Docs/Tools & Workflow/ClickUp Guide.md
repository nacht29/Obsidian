## What Is ClickUp?

ClickUp is an all-in-one productivity and task management platform (similar to Jira, Asana, Trello).

At Data Flows (a software house), ClickUp is used as a **two-way platform between us and our clients** — not just internal project management. Activities include:

- Clients requesting status updates on features
- Clients requesting clarification on features
- Developers updating ticket status (e.g. pending QA, completed)
- Clients requesting changes to features

---

## Communication Best Practices

Since we communicate directly with clients through ClickUp, all updates must be **clear, professional, and safe**.

### Use simple, direct, and specific language
- ✅ Good: "Customers can reset their password through the 'Forgot Password' link on the login page."
- ❌ Bad: "Just click there if you can't remember."

### Use professional and polite language
- ✅ Good: "App users can view their user profile by pressing this button."
- ❌ Bad: "Just click there lor if you can't remember your thingy."

### Check with team before giving deadlines
- ✅ Good: "I'll discuss this with the team and provide an update by [day + time]."
- ❌ Bad: "I can definitely deliver this by Friday 5pm."

### Avoid programming terms and jargon
- ✅ Good: "The report will automatically refresh every morning with the latest data."
- ❌ Bad: "We use a CRON job scheduled at 06:00 server time to trigger a data pipeline."
- Note: Some clients may have their own dev teams — adjust technical depth accordingly.

### Never make promises to clients
- ✅ Good: "We'll review your request and provide an estimated timeline once confirmed by our team."
- ❌ Bad: "We can finish this for you by next week without any issues."
