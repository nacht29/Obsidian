# Software & Laptop Policy

Data Flows Software and Laptop Usage Policy. Source: Doc 5.

---

## Purpose

To ensure the secure, legal, and efficient use of employees' laptops and software — protecting company data, preventing malware, and maintaining software licensing compliance.

---

## Software Usage

- Only **licensed software** may be installed on work laptops
- Pirated or unlicensed software is strictly prohibited (illegal and exposes Company to criminal prosecution)
- Unlicensed software often contains malware that can damage devices and compromise data
- Company provides and pays for required licensed software
- To install additional software: submit a request to department head for **prior approval**
- Do not modify or uninstall essential security or productivity software
- All software licenses and product keys are **company property** — do not share externally

---

## Security Incident Reporting

Immediately report any actual or suspected incident to: **security@dataflows.co**

Examples of incidents to report:
- Lost or stolen device
- Malware infection
- Successful phishing attempt
- Suspicious network activity
- Accidental data disclosure

---

## SentinelOne Agent

All employees must install the **SentinelOne Agent** on their work device.

## Monthly Antivirus Scan (Mandatory)

All full-time employees must:
1. Perform an antivirus scan on their device **at least once a month** using the company-provided antivirus software
2. Ensure the device generates a full **"safe" report**
3. **Submit the completed scan report to HR** for record-keeping

Failure to comply may result in restricted system access or disciplinary action (Handbook Section 10.6).

---

## Data Segregation & Protection

- All company data must be accessed, edited, and stored **only in authorized company systems** (internal servers, company-approved cloud storage, secure virtual environments)
- **Do NOT** save sensitive company files on your local unmanaged hard drive (Desktop, Downloads, personal cloud folders)
- Company data must be **encrypted** where applicable (e.g. full disk encryption as required by CSA compliance check)
- Refer to DF IT Security Policy for full details
- Company holds **no responsibility** for loss or corruption of personal data stored on employee devices

---

## Physical Security & Maintenance

- **Lock** device when unattended in public or at home
- Store device securely (e.g. locked cabinet) when not in use
- **Immediately report** lost or stolen device as a security incident
- Never share your passwords or login details
- Never save company passwords in non-approved browser managers or sticky notes
- Keep OS and personal software **up to date** with vendor patches to minimize vulnerabilities
