# WFH & Away Requests

Procedures for requesting additional WFH days or short away during working hours. Source: Doc 8.

**Standard WFH days:** Wednesday & Friday (no request needed for these).

---

## Additional WFH Requests

**Form:** https://forms.gle/g9HEi7yxeo13D8bo9

### Eligibility
- Must have a valid reason (medical appointment, home emergency, personal obligation)
- Also allowed if supervisor determines it is appropriate (e.g. following a late-night deployment)

### Procedure
1. Inform supervisor **at least 2 hours before start of working hours**
2. Seek approval from supervisor
3. Once approved, notify in the **Office Group Chat**
4. Fill in the form

### Responsibilities
- Employee must ensure all work responsibilities, deliverables, and deadlines are met
- Supervisors monitor employee work and ensure communication stays clear
- If a supervisor finds a WFH employee underperforming (slow responses, missed tasks), the supervisor has authority to **revoke the WFH arrangement**

---

## Away Requests (1–2 Hour Partial Absences)

For short absences during working hours (e.g. personal errands, doctor visits).

### Eligibility
- Must have a valid reason
- Applies only to **1–2 hour** partial absences
- Work adjustments (rescheduling/delegation) must be agreed with supervisor

### Procedure
1. Inform supervisor **at least 2 hours before start of working hours**
2. Seek approval from supervisor
3. Once approved, notify in the **Office Group Chat** about the absence hours
4. **Make up the absent hours** by adjusting/expanding working hours on the same day

### Responsibilities
- Employee ensures tasks and responsibilities are completed on time despite the absence
- Supervisors monitor work and ensure clear communication
