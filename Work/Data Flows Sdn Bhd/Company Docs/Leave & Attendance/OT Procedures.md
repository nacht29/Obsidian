## When Overtime May Be Scheduled

Overtime may be requested by supervisor in these situations:
- **Deployment-related tasks** (e.g. performing deployment 10:00 pm – 11:00 pm)
- **Customer support during off-hours** (e.g. after 8:00 pm on weekdays, or any time on weekends/holidays)
- **Special HOD requests** (e.g. meeting project timelines)

---

## OT Formula

```
OT Pay = Basic Salary ÷ 26 days ÷ 8 hours × 1.5 × Total OT Hours
```

---

## Payroll Cut-Off

- Claims by **22nd of the month** → paid in the **same month's** payslip
- Claims **after 22nd** → paid in the **following month's** payslip

---

## OT Request Procedure

1. **Get supervisor approval** before starting overtime
2. **Submit OT Request Form:** https://forms.gle/mzwnP25WptD89yoW7
3. Fill in all required details in the form

> **Tip:** Submit all OT dates for the month **in one submission before the 22nd** rather than multiple entries.
