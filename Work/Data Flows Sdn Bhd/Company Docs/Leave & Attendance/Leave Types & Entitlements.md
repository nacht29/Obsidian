# Leave Types & Entitlements

Full leave entitlements from the Staff Handbook (Section 4). For how to apply, see [[Work/Data Flows Sdn Bhd/Company Docs/Leave & Attendance/Leave Procedures|Leave Procedures]].

---

## Annual Leave

| Length of Service | Entitlement |
|------------------|------------|
| < 1 year | 15 days |
| 1 – 2 years | 17 days |
| 2 – 4 years | 19 days |
| 4 years and above | 22 days |

- Calculated on calendar year (1 Jan – 31 Dec); pro-rated for joiners mid-year
- Carry forward: max **4 days**, must be used by 31 March of following year; excess forfeited
- Apply **at least 7 days in advance**
- Company may recall employee from leave if operationally required (leave reinstated)
- Annual leave balance may offset notice period on resignation at Company's discretion

---

## Half Day Leave

- Either AM or PM; deducted as 0.5 day from annual leave
- Employee must work **at least 4 hours** (excluding 1-hour break)

---

## Medical Leave

| Length of Service | Entitlement |
|------------------|------------|
| < 2 years | 14 days |
| 2 – 5 years | 18 days |
| 5 years and above | 22 days |

- Must be certified by a registered medical practitioner
- Inform supervisor **at least 3 hours before** working hours
- Submit via HRMS with MC attached **on the same day**; failing this it becomes Emergency Leave → deducted from Annual Leave
- Online clinic MCs **not accepted**
- COVID-19: must submit RTK Antigen/PCR report
- Unused medical leave is **not** carried forward; not encashable

---

## Hospitalisation Leave

- All employees: **60 days** per calendar year (includes rest days and public holidays during hospitalization)
- First 30 days: full pay; days 31–60: half pay
- Must submit original MC to HR within **24 hours** of commencement

---

## Public Holidays

- Follow Federal and State gazetted holidays (KL and Sabah schedules differ)
- Absent on the working day immediately before/after a public holiday without approval = forfeit that holiday pay

---

## Marriage Leave

- **3 consecutive working days** (confirmed employees only; first legal marriage only)
- Apply **at least 7 days in advance**; must be within 2 days before/after wedding date
- Submit: Marriage Certificate (for registry date) or Wedding Invitation + Marriage Certificate (for ceremony date)

---

## Maternity Leave

- **98 consecutive days** per confinement; up to **5 children**
- Eligible if employed ≥ 90 days in the 9 months before confinement
- Must notify Company/supervisor **at least 4 months** before expected confinement
- Cannot use maternity leave as notice period for resignation

---

## Paternity Leave

- **7 consecutive days** per confinement; up to **5 confinements**
- Eligible after **12 months** of service
- Commences on day of child's birth
- Notify Company/supervisor **at least 60 days** before expected confinement
- Submit birth certificate to HR within **2 weeks** of birth

---

## Compassionate Leave

| Type | Relationship | Entitlement |
|------|-------------|------------|
| Compassionate I | Immediate family (parents, spouse, siblings, parents-in-law, grandparents, children) | 5 consecutive working days |
| Compassionate II | Non-direct family / relatives | 2 consecutive working days |

- Must submit death certificate; leave date must be on or within 2 days after death date
- Apply within **24 hours** of death

---

## Birthday Leave

- **1 day** for confirmed employees on their birthday
- If required to work on birthday, may take another day within the same birthday month
- Must apply as **full day** (no half-day); must apply **at least 14 days in advance**
- Not applicable during probation; not carried forward; cannot offset notice period

---

## Emergency Leave

- For unavoidable, unexpected circumstances (accident, death of non-immediate relative, serious family illness, etc.)
- Deducted from Annual Leave (or Unpaid Leave if AL exhausted)
- **Limit: 3 days** per calendar year; beyond 3 days = Unpaid Leave + possible disciplinary action
- Notify supervisor **at least 2 hours** before scheduled start
- < 4 hours = half-day deduction; > 4 hours but < 1 day = 1 full day deduction
- Must submit supporting documents upon return

---

## "Short Away" Notice

- For personal errands < **1 hour** during working hours
- Must get **explicit** verbal approval from manager before leaving (notification alone ≠ approval)
- Must notify DF group chat with: duration, reason, expected return time
- Must make up the time on the same day
- > 1 hour = must apply half-day or full-day leave via HRMS
- All instances are recorded; frequent use may affect performance review

---

## Unpaid Leave

- When Annual Leave is exhausted
- Apply **at least 7 days in advance**; granted at Company's sole discretion
- **Max 7 days total within one year**
- Deducted from same month's salary if taken 1st–22nd; reflected in following month if after 22nd

---

## No-Pay Leave (Absenteeism)

- Absent without approval → automatically no-pay + possible disciplinary action
