# Leave Procedures

How to apply for leave at Data Flows. Source: Leave Procedures doc (Doc 4).

---

## Annual Leave & Other Planned Leave

1. **Apply in Kakitangan** — all leave applications submitted through the Kakitangan HR system
2. **Supervisor Approval** — direct supervisor reviews and approves/rejects
3. **Update Google Calendar** — once approved, record leave in the DF Google Calendar
4. **Subscribe to DF Google Calendar:**
   https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=c_b5rjrsu2r3udcfkib0u3lhpr18%40group.calendar.google.com&ctz=Asia%2FSingapore
5. HR will consolidate leave records and share weekly updates in the WhatsApp group chat

---

## Medical Leave / Emergency Leave (Same Day)

1. **Inform supervisor** — notify direct supervisor as soon as possible
2. **Notify in WhatsApp group chat** — update the team so everyone is aware
3. **Apply in Kakitangan** — submit the leave request and attach the supporting document (e.g. medical certificate)

---

## General Rules

- All leave must be informed to supervisor and in the group chat **at least 1 hour in advance** for unplanned absences
- Approved leave must be recorded in the DF Calendar
- Submit via HRMS with relevant attachments for official record
