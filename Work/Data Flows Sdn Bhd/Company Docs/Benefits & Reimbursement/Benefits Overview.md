# Benefits Overview

Employee benefits for confirmed Data Flows staff (Handbook Section 5). All benefits are for confirmed employees unless stated otherwise.

---

## 5.1 Medical, Dental, Optometry & Lifestyle Reimbursement

Annual reimbursement by job grade:

| Job Grade | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| DF6 | RM 1,500 |
| DF4 & DF5 | RM 1,200 |
| DF2 & DF3 | RM 1,000 |
| DF1 | RM 600 |

**Claimable items:**

| Category | Items |
|----------|-------|
| Medical | GP consultations, specialist treatment, health screening |
| Dental | Extraction, filling, scaling, X-ray, check-up, consultation |
| Optometry | Spectacles, contact lenses |
| Lifestyle | Gym, travel (flight tickets), beauty & spa |

- Pro-rated from month of confirmation to 31 December for new confirmations mid-year
- Submit via HRMS in PDF format; deadline: **22nd of each month** (after 22nd goes to next payroll)
- Unused balance **not** carried forward to next year
- Employees serving notice period **cannot** claim remaining balance

## 5.2 Laptop Purchase Reimbursement

One-time claim for newly confirmed permanent employees.

| Job Grade | Maximum Claim |
|-----------|--------------|
| DF5 & DF6 | RM 6,000 |
| DF1, DF2, DF3 & DF4 | RM 3,000 |

- Claim amount: **70% of purchase price OR grade limit — whichever is lower**
- Eligibility window: **6 months from first day of employment** (extended if probation is extended)
- Laptop is **fully owned by employee**; company has no warranty/service responsibility
- Only the laptop itself is claimable (no accessories: keyboard, mouse, headphones, etc.)
- **Clawback:** If employee leaves within **12 months** of purchase date, must reimburse on pro-rated basis
  - Formula: `Claim Amount – [(Claim Amount ÷ 365) × Days Served from Purchase Date]`
