# Expense Claims

How to submit and what qualifies for expense reimbursement (Handbook Section 7).

---

## Submission Rules

- All claims must have **original** receipts/bills/invoices in **PDF format only** (no screenshots or image files)
- Receipts must clearly show: date, amount, vendor, and description
- Submit via Company's HRMS system with supervisor confirmation/approval
- All claims must be submitted **within the same month** as the purchase date
- **Deadline: 22nd of each month** — requests after 22nd are processed in the next payroll cycle
- Once approved, reimbursement is paid together with that month's salary

### Mileage "Closest Office Rule"
When travelling to a customer site:
- If the site is **closer to your home** than the office → claim distance from **home to site**
- If the site is **closer to the office** than your home → claim distance from **office to site**
- Attach a screenshot showing the distance when submitting
