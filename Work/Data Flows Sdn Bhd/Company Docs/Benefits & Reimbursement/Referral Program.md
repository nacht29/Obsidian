# Referral Program

Data Flows Employee Referral Program. Source: Doc 9 & Handbook Section 5.9.

---

## How It Works

Refer a qualified candidate to join Data Flows. If your referral is hired **and successfully completes their probationary period**, you receive **RM 500** as a reward.

**Open positions & referral form:** https://forms.gle/fx4Q5B2Q3K2jo7VT9

---

## Rules

- **Referring employee must personally know the candidate** they are referring
- No limit on the number of referrals you can make
- For **duplicate referrals**, the first person to refer the candidate gets the incentive
- **Referring employee must still be employed** (not on notice period) when the incentive is paid
- Hiring managers and HR employees are **not eligible** for the referral incentive
- Exercise caution when referring candidates from previous employers — may violate non-solicitation/non-compete agreements
