# Password Policy

New Password Protection Policy (effective 26 Sept 2025).

---

## Best Practices

- Send passwords **via email only** (not WhatsApp); always CC team leader/manager
- Send passwords **individually** — do not include multiple passwords in one email
- Use **high-strength passwords** for all accounts
- Record passwords in Google Doc or Google Sheet (so company can revoke access if needed)
- Enable **2FA** (e.g. Google Authenticator) on all logins whenever possible
- When sending passwords to clients, send to **authorized individuals only** — do not loop in everyone

---

## For Dev Work

- **Backend projects:** Create separate admin users per team member to differentiate logins
- **Production access:** Check with manager/team lead; only grant when absolutely required. Formal email request required.
- **Production environment:** Do not make data changes directly unless there is written (black-and-white) client approval
- **Production database:** Do not download the production DB to local environment; consult manager/senior for debugging approach
- **Links and materials:** Grant access to materials individually, only to those who need it

---

## Avoid These

- Do NOT write passwords in Notepad
- Do NOT use simple/weak passwords (e.g. "password", "123456")
- Do NOT share passwords via WhatsApp
- Do NOT share credentials with anyone who requests them without written approval from manager/team lead via email
- Do NOT grant public access to materials or links that make them accessible to anyone outside the company
