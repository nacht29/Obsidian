## Performance Management

**PIP (Performance Improvement Plan):** Activated for poor performers. Not a punishment — gives reasonable time with guidance. Consistent failure can lead to disciplinary action including termination.

**Coaching:** A manager/team lead works with the employee on specific skills, goals, and development. Tailored to individual strengths and weaknesses.

---

## Code of Conduct

**Minor misconduct examples:** poor timekeeping, negligence, improper use of company property, failing to adhere to safety procedures.

**Major misconduct examples:** theft, fraud, dishonesty, drinking alcohol during work hours, gambling on premises, spreading false rumours, sexual harassment, bribery, fighting/assault, divulging confidential information, absenteeism without reason.

**Disciplinary process:** Show-cause notice → domestic inquiry → suspension (up to **14 working days** at half pay; moves to full pay after 14 working days if inquiry continues) → termination/dismissal.

**Sexual harassment:** Zero tolerance. Covers quid pro quo and hostile environment types. Incidents to be reported to HR or management.

**Whistle Blowing:** Report **in writing** to the **Managing Director** (the designated oversight person). Verbal reports are not accepted.

**Anti-Bribery / Anti-Corruption & AML:** Strictly prohibited. Employees must avoid giving or receiving gifts that could be perceived as a bribe. Business meals or hospitality must be reasonable, proportionate, and transparently recorded.

## Employee Privacy & IT

- No recording devices/camera phones in unauthorized areas.
- Company email and internet are for work purposes; monitored.
- Only licensed software; pirated software is prohibited.
- Must install SentinelOne Agent on work device.
- Report security incidents immediately to security@dataflows.co.
- Company data must not be stored on personal/unmanaged local drives.
- Data must be encrypted where applicable.

---

## Separation of Employment

**Resignation notice period:** As stated in Letter of Employment. Annual leave may offset notice period at Company's discretion.

**Retirement:** At age as defined by Company policy or applicable law.

**Exit interview:** Conducted before last working day.

**Return of company property:** All company assets (access cards, equipment, documents) must be returned on last day.

**Deletion of confidential info:** Employees must delete/return all confidential company information upon termination.

---

## Grievance Procedure

Employees with a grievance may raise it with their immediate supervisor, then HOD, and finally HR Department if unresolved.
