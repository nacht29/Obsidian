# Meeting Room SOP

Standard Operating Procedure for meeting room usage at Data Flows. Source: Doc 12.

---

## Booking Policy

- All meeting rooms must be booked in advance via **Google Calendar** (Google Meet system)
- When creating a calendar event, you must:
  - Select an available meeting room
  - Include meeting time, duration, and participants

> **Rule:** If it's not booked in Google Calendar, it is **not reserved**.

---

## Room Usage Rules

- Meeting rooms are for **official business purposes only**
- Do not "block" or reserve rooms without an actual meeting planned
- Use the room **only during your booked time slot**

---

## Time Management

- Start and end meetings **on time**
- If meeting ends early, release the room by updating or cancelling the booking
- Do not extend meetings beyond your scheduled time if another booking exists

---

## No Abuse Policy

- Meeting rooms must not be used as personal workspaces or break areas
- Unauthorized occupation (sitting without a booking) is considered **misuse**

---

## Room Etiquette

- Keep the room clean and organized after use
- Return furniture and equipment to original positions
- Turn off lights, screens, and devices when leaving
