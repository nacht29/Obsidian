# Workplace Etiquette & Safety

Office etiquette and safety rules for all Data Flows employees. Source: Doc 11.

---

## Cleanliness & Personal Responsibility

- Clean up your own workspace before leaving
- Wash and return used cups, plates, and utensils
- Do not leave trash on desks, in the pantry, or in shared areas
- Keep common areas (pantry, meeting rooms) clean after use — if you make a mess, clean it immediately
- Do not throw food or waste into the sink (to avoid clogging)
- Keep the toilet clean and dry after use
- **Fridge rules:** No strong-smelling food (e.g. durian); label your items; clear food within **1 day**
- Wash and keep your own utensils properly after use

---

## Equipment & Energy Responsibility

- Turn off lights when leaving a room (if no one else is inside)
- Switch off air-conditioning, fans, and electrical equipment after use
- Shut down or lock your computer before leaving
- Do not leave devices running unnecessarily
- Switch on air-conditioning **only when the area is occupied**
- When switching off air-conditioning: **turn it off using the remote/control first**, then switch off the main power

---

## Office Equipment Use

- Use shared equipment (microwave, etc.) responsibly
- Report faulty equipment immediately
- Do not misuse or overload power sockets
- Return items to their proper place after use

---

## Safety & Security

- Lock doors or secure areas when required
- Do not allow unauthorized persons into the office
- Keep personal belongings and valuables safe
- Report suspicious activity immediately

---

## General Safety Practices

- Keep walkways and exits clear at all times
- Do not block fire exits or safety equipment
- Follow emergency procedures when instructed
- Report hazards (spills, exposed wires, etc.) immediately

---

## Workplace Etiquette

- Be considerate of others — noise, cleanliness, use of shared space
- Respect shared facilities and office property
- Avoid leaving personal items in common areas
- Help maintain a comfortable and safe environment for everyone
