# Supply Chain Terms

| Term           | Full Name                              | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| PO             | Purchase Order                         | Buyer's request to a supplier for goods — "we want to buy X units of Y."                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| SO             | Sales Order                            | Seller's internal record that goods are owed to a buyer — mirror of a PO from the seller's side. In this project, STOs ride on the SO tables.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| STO            | Stock Transfer Order                   | Internal movement request between two of the same company's locations (e.g. DC → store) — no sale, no invoice.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| GR             | Goods Receipt                          | Confirmation that ordered goods physically arrived and were checked into inventory.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| GI             | Goods Issue                            | Confirmation that goods physically left a location (shipped out, consumed, or transferred).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ASN            | Advance Shipping Notice                | Supplier's notice sent before goods arrive, listing what's coming and when.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| SKU            | Stock Keeping Unit                     | Unique identifier for one specific sellable product variant.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| DC             | Distribution Center                    | Warehouse that stores and redistributes goods to stores/customers, rather than selling direct.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ETA / ETD      | Estimated Time of Arrival / Departure  | Expected receive date / expected ship date for an order line.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Lead Time      | —                                      | Time between placing an order and receiving the goods.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Safety Stock   | —                                      | Buffer inventory held to absorb demand or supply variability.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Reorder Point  | —                                      | Inventory level that triggers a new replenishment order.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Backorder      | —                                      | Demand that can't be filled now because stock is insufficient; carried forward until fulfilled.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Allocation     | —                                      | Reserving on-hand stock against a specific demand line, making it unavailable to other orders.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Cycle Count    | —                                      | Periodic partial physical inventory count (vs. a full annual count) to catch stock discrepancies.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| FIFO / LIFO    | First In First Out / Last In First Out | Inventory consumption order — FIFO is standard for perishables/shelf-life-sensitive goods (used in this project).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| WMS            | Warehouse Management System            | Software that runs warehouse operations — inventory, picking, receiving (the source system here).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ERP            | Enterprise Resource Planning           | Company-wide system of record (finance, procurement, inventory) — SAP in this project's SiteCode/CompanyCode fields.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 3PL            | Third-Party Logistics                  | Outsourced provider handling warehousing/shipping on a company's behalf.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| On-Hand Qty    | —                                      | Physical stock currently sitting in a location, regardless of allocation status.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Available Qty  | —                                      | On-hand minus allocated/reserved/suspended — what's actually free to promise to new demand.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| In-Transit Qty | —                                      | Stock shipped but not yet received at destination.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| UOM            | Unit of Measure                        | How a quantity of an item is counted (each, case, pallet, kg, etc.). Same SKU often has multiple UOMs with different physical dimensions (a case ≠ an each).<br>In T1: `item_uom` CTE, joined via `QuantityUM` — used to get `DisplayUM` and compute per-unit Length × Width × Height for `TotalM3` (line 243). Without knowing the UOM, `TotalQty` alone is meaningless for volume — 10 "each" ≠ 10 "cases." |
| QC             | Quality Control                        | Inspection process before/during outbound shipping — checking an item isn't damaged, expired, wrong, etc. A failed/held item gets flagged with a reason.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
