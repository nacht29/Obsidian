
## Switch between Gemini and Google Assistant

### Enable through your device's settings:
1. Navigate to **Settings**
2. Go to **Apps**. 
3. Select the **Assist app**.
4. Choose **Gemini**.

### Enable through the Google app:
1. Open the **Google app**.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Select **Google Assistant**.
4. Choose **Digital assistants from Google**.
5. Select **Gemini** and follow the instructions.
