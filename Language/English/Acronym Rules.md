## Definitions

### Abbreviation
A general term for any shortened word or phrase. It can be a single truncated word (like "Prof." for professor) or a larger phrase.

### Initialism
An abbreviation made from the first letters of a group of words, where you pronounce each letter individually (like "FBI" or "ATM").

### Acronym
An initialism that forms a new word you pronounce as a single word rather than spelling out the letters (like "NATO" or "NASA"). All acronyms are initialisms and abbreviations, but not vice versa.

### Mnemonic
A broader memory aid or technique used to help recall information. An acronym can be a mnemonic (like using "SMART" for goals), but mnemonics also include rhymes, songs, or mental images that do not rely on word creation.

## TL;DR

All abbreviation is an acronym, but not all acronyms are abbreviations. Acronym describes any shortening of a word, while others describe a specific way of shortening a word.
